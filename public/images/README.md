# High Skill Taekwondo Academy - Image Guide

This folder contains all the photos and visual media used across the website.
Replaceable placeholders are included so the website looks great out of the box.

## How to Add Your Own Photos

You can replace any placeholder by adding your own JPG, PNG, or WebP photo with the exact same name, or by changing the path in `src/data/academyData.ts`.

### 1. Home Images (`public/images/home/`)
- `hero-bg.svg` (Recommended: 1920x1080 or 1400x800) - Main hero banner showing your dojang, team, or head instructor.
- `training-feature.svg` (Recommended: 800x600) - Junior students training or kicking pads.
- `poomsae-feature.svg` (Recommended: 800x600) - Teens or adults practicing forms or sparring.
- `kids-feature.svg` (Recommended: 800x600) - Little Tigers kids class in session.

### 2. Coach Photos (`public/images/coach/`)
- `head-coach.svg` (Recommended: 600x750 portrait) - Master / Head Instructor photo in uniform (Dobok).
- `assistant-coach.svg` (Recommended: 600x750 portrait) - Senior Instructor / Assistant Coach photo.

### 3. Gallery Photos (`public/images/gallery/`)
- `gallery-1.svg` to `gallery-8.svg` (Recommended: 800x600)
- Add more photos anytime! You can register new photos in `src/data/academyData.ts` under the `GALLERY_ITEMS` array.

### 4. Achievement Photos (`public/images/achievements/`)
- `trophy-state.svg` (800x600) - Team trophy or tournament medal presentation.
- `best-academy-award.svg` (800x600) - Academy recognition certificate or award.
- `black-belt-graduation.svg` (800x600) - Dan black belt graduation group photo.
- `national-championship.svg` (800x600) - Championship demonstration squad.
